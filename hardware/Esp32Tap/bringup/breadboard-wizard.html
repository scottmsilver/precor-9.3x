Safety & final inspection

DPDT lug arrangements vary. Meter-identify both independent common/throw pairs; never infer contacts from physical position.

If either rail is split or fails continuity, replace it with one verified continuous rail segment. Do not assume continuity and do not bridge it.

  • Confirm no 5V, native USB, or treadmill connection.
  • Verify every resistor value and both LED A/K polarities.
  • Baseline: DPDT ungrounded, GPIO6 open, GPIO7 open.
