Esp32Tap Breadboard — Point-to-Point Wiring List

Each row is one physical connection. Map the RJ45 breakout terminals and SOT-23 adapters with a meter before using their labels.

Start with all power disconnected. Leave STANDALONE POWER and COIL POWER removed. The relay datasheet pin drawing is a bottom view—meter-verify K1 before wiring.
