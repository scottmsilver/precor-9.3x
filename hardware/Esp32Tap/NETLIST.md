## Key wiring facts

* **Fail-safe relay K1** (Omron G6K-2F-Y, coil 1/8, pole A COM=3 NC=2 NO=4,
  pole B COM=6 NC=7 NO=5): de-energized, both poles bridge console pin 6
  (CONS6) to motor pin 6 (MOT6) **and physically disconnect the ESP32 TX**
  (which sits on the NO contacts).  Unpowered/crashed board = stock
  treadmill.  Energized (Emulate only), MOT6 is driven by ESP TX via R6
  and the console line is released (only the 4.7k RX tap remains on it).
* **RX taps are 4.7k** (R7 console pin 6, R8 pin 3) so a dead 3.3V rail
  back-feeds <0.3mA through the GPIO clamp diodes — bus levels intact.
* **ESD clamps D5/D6/D7 are PESD3V3L1BA to GND only** (bidirectional,
  not rail-referenced) — inert when the board is unpowered.
* **Pass-through pins**: RJ45 1,2,4,5,7,8 are pure copper between J1 and
  J2 (pin 5 safety interlock and unknown pin 4 never touch silicon).
* **VIN ORing**: 8V rail (D1) or USB VBUS (D2) feed the TPS54202 buck —
  bench flashing needs no treadmill.
* GPIO map: IO17=UART1 TX (motor pin6), IO18=UART1 RX (console pin6),
  IO16=UART2 RX (pin3 tap), IO21=relay driver, IO38=status LED.
  None are ESP32-S3 strap pins (0/3/45/46) or the USB pins (19/20).
